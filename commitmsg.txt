Fix drawings not translating between timeframes, and giant position boxes

timeScale().timeToCoordinate() only answers for a time that is EXACTLY a bar
time in the series. A drawing viewed on another timeframe almost never is: a
rectangle corner at 14:43 is not a 30m bar time, and a daily drawing stores
exactly UTC midnight, which is not an intraday bar time either. Measured on
the real drawings file, 12 of 14 corners returned null on the 30m chart.

Those nulls fell through to a fallback chain that clamped a corner to the left
or right edge, or interpolated with Number(vr.from) - which is NaN on every
daily/weekly chart, since their visible range is "2026-11-05", not a number.
So a rectangle became a full-width band, a zero-width sliver, or nothing; and a
position whose left corner clamped to 0 while its right corner resolved spanned
the entire chart.

Map time to the chart's fractional BAR INDEX instead, against the series own
times, then index to pixel. logicalToCoordinate is exactly affine in the index,
is defined outside the loaded data (so a shape reaching into the future keeps
its real width instead of pinning to the edge), and behaves the same on numeric
and business-day charts. It returns 0 for a FRACTIONAL index, so the transform
is rebuilt from two integer probes. Verified to agree with timeToCoordinate to
<1e-6 on every real bar - a strict superset of what worked before.

Also:
- Align day-level times. UTC midnight of day D is 19:00 ET on D-1, so a daily
  drawing read as an instant landed a whole session early; it now maps to 00:00
  ET of that date (DST-safe, offset derived per date). Going the other way, an
  intraday time is bucketed by its ET date, not its UTC date, which used to put
  anything drawn after 19:00 ET on the following trading day.
- xToTimeAnchored goes through index space too. coordinateToTime snaps to the
  nearest bar, so dragging across an overnight gap jumped the time by hours, and
  the old px-to-seconds extrapolation past the last bar could fling a drawing
  months away - there are stored rectangles spanning -329 days from this.
- Floor shape widths at MIN_SHAPE_W. A 20-minute position is 0.014 of a daily
  bar and used to draw nothing at all, so it looked like the drawing was lost.
